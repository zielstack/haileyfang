Hailey Fang

Hailey Fang

U.S. Regional Director • NOT JUST A LABEL

In 2014, Hailey Fang founded a startup fashion brand in China, dedicated to creating modern women's fashion. With a clear design vision and dedication to quality, she led the creative direction and developed multiple seasonal collections, bringing modern aesthetic sensibility and refined silhouettes to contemporary womenswear.

Throughout her career, Hailey has managed end-to-end business operations spanning design direction, material sourcing, and garment production. She established enduring relationships with premium suppliers and managed small-batch manufacturing to maintain strict quality and exclusivity across every collection.

Currently residing in Los Angeles, California, Hailey operates the apparel design company NOT JUST A LABEL and serves as its U.S. Regional Director. In this role, she oversees brand marketing, drives online sales channel development, and leads full-chain operations, connecting the brand's fashion collections with discerning clients worldwide.

Company & Operations

NOT JUST A LABEL

End-to-End Excellence

NOT JUST A LABEL is an apparel design company operated by Hailey Fang, dedicated to delivering contemporary women's fashion with an emphasis on quality craftsmanship, thoughtful design, and modern elegance.

Since founding her first fashion brand in China in 2014, Hailey has cultivated enduring supplier partnerships and established a disciplined small-batch production workflow. From initial conceptualization and textile sourcing to pattern making and final garment assembly, every phase is managed with precision to ensure product excellence.

Headquartered at 300 S Grand Ave in Los Angeles, California, NOT JUST A LABEL integrates creative design direction with modern digital marketing and online sales channels, serving clients seeking timeless, well-crafted fashion pieces.
